Some historical archives and compiled satellite objects are referenced but not included. In particular, the initial repository does not claim to contain the exact Phase60 C sources or a flashable satellite build. The source available here is the emulator, its tools and original firmware-contract probes.

The current scope is in ROADMAP.md: APRS TX in the first usable version, APRS RX in the second stage, DMR a required subsequent goal.
